Selection basis: each training run selects its model family on the validation split. This comparison uses the selected models' untouched test metrics, ranked by Fraud F1 first, then fraud recall, precision, PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, and training time for practical ties. Accuracy is not used for setup recommendation.
